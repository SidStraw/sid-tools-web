## Where twelve points went

Pick a before and an after. The scores come first as rings, then every metric as a row. A change too small to mean anything is labelled as noise instead of being dressed up as a delta, which keeps the rows that did move easy to find.

- Performance 92 to 42, and the metric rows that account for it

- Page weight 125 KB to 1.68 MB, with the resource type responsible

- The audits that flipped, with the saving each one measured

## How to compare two Lighthouse reports

- Open the monitor for the page you care about.

- Choose a run from before the change and a run from after it.

- Read the rows marked as moved: the metrics first, then the audits that flipped and the requests that grew.

Without picking anything

## The run explains itself before you ask

Choosing two runs is for when you want to look. When a run drops eight points or more on its own, it arrives already compared: the report carries a panel written against the median of recent runs, so the reason is on the page when you open it.

Performance fell from 92 to 42 against the run of 5 Sept. LCP went from 2.6 s to 10.7 s, TBT from 0 ms to 2.5 s. Image grew by 1.57 MB. Audits that flipped: Improve image delivery, Reduce JavaScript execution time, Minimize main-thread work.The panel as it appeared on the demo workspace, unedited.
